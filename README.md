# Ease Estate

## Introduction

**Ease Estate** is a comprehensive room booking application designed to streamline the property rental experience. Built with cutting-edge technology, the platform provides seamless booking management, real-time analytics, and an intuitive user interface for both guests and administrators.

### What Ease Estate Does

Ease Estate empowers property managers and hosts to:
- Manage room listings across multiple categories (Standard, Deluxe, Suites, Specialty)
- Track bookings in real-time with detailed status management
- Monitor financial performance through advanced analytics dashboards
- Communicate with guests through automated alerts and admin announcements
- Handle user favorites and preferences efficiently

For guests, Ease Estate enables:
- Browse and book available rooms with flexible date selection
- View room details, pricing, and availability
- Manage bookings with status tracking
- Save favorite rooms for quick access
- Receive booking notifications and updates
- View personal booking history

## Room Management

### Adding a New Room

1. From the **Admin Dashboard**, tap **"+ Add New Room"** button
2. Fill in the following details:
   - **Room Title** - Name of the room (e.g., "Ocean View Suite")
   - **Category** - Select from Standard, Deluxe, Suites, or Specialty
   - **Description** - Detailed description of room features
   - **Price per Night** - Enter the nightly rate
   - **Facilities** - List amenities (e.g., "WiFi, AC, Hot Water")

3. **Upload Room Image**
   - Tap the image area to select a photo from your gallery
   - Choose a high-quality image showing the room clearly
   - Recommended: Landscape orientation, minimum 1200x800px

4. Tap **"Add Room"** to save
5. The room will be immediately available for bookings

### Editing an Existing Room

1. From the **Admin Dashboard**, find the room card you want to edit
2. Tap the **"Edit"** button on the room card
3. Update any of the following:
   - Room Title
   - Description
   - Price per Night
   - Facilities
   - Room Image

4. Tap **"Update Room"** to save changes
5. Changes are reflected immediately across the platform

### Deleting a Room

1. From the **Admin Dashboard**, find the room you want to delete
2. Tap the **"Delete"** button on the room card
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted
4. The room is removed from listings and becomes unavailable for new bookings

## Booking Management

### Accessing Bookings

1. From the **Admin Dashboard**, tap **"Booking Management"** button
2. You will see the **Booking Status** page with all received bookings

### Booking Information Displayed

For each booking, you can see:
- **Booking ID** - Unique identifier for the booking
- **Room Image** - Preview of the booked room
- **Check-in & Check-out Dates** - Reservation period
- **Total Price** - Amount charged for the stay
- **Current Status** - Current booking status
- **Status Dropdown** - For updating the booking status

### Managing Booking Status

Available booking statuses:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting admin review |
| **Approved** | Approved but awaiting confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | Confirmed reservation |
| **Completed** | Finished stay |
| **Rejected** | Rejected booking (automatically deleted) |

### How to Update Booking Status

1. Navigate to the **Booking Status** page
2. Find the booking you want to update
3. Tap the **Status Dropdown** menu
4. Select the new status from the options
5. The system will:
   - Update the booking in the database
   - Create an automated alert for the user
   - Update analytics data in real-time

### Automated Alerts

When you change a booking status, the user automatically receives an alert:
- **Rejected bookings** - "Booking Rejected" notification
- **Approved bookings** - "Booking Status Updated to Approved" notification
- **Confirmed bookings** - "Booking Status Updated to Confirmed" notification
- **Completed bookings** - "Booking Status Updated to Completed" notification

Users can view these alerts in their **Alerts section**

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

   - **Password** - Strong password (minimum 6 characters recommended)
   - **Confirm Password** - Verify your password

3. Tap **"Register"** to create your account
4. You will be automatically logged in and taken to the home page

### Logging In

1. On the **Login page**, enter your credentials:
   - **Email** - Your registered email address
   - **Password** - Your account password

2. Tap **"Sign In"** to access your account

### Quick Login with Google

1. Tap **"Sign in with Google"** button
2. Select your Google account
3. You will be logged in instantly with your Google credentials

## Browsing & Booking

### Viewing Room Details

1. From the **Home page**, tap on any room card
2. You will see the **Room Details page** with:
   - Large room image
   - Complete description
   - Full list of facilities
   - Price per night
   - **"Book Now"** button

### Making a Booking

1. On the **Room Details page**, tap **"Book Now"**
2. You will enter the **Booking page** with:
   - Room information at the top
   - Check-in date selector
   - Check-out date selector
   - Total price calculator

#### Selecting Dates

1. **Check-in Date**:
   - Tap the check-in date field
   - A calendar will appear
   - Select your desired check-in date
   - Dates in the past cannot be selected

2. **Check-out Date**:
   - Tap the check-out date field
   - Select your check-out date
   - Must be after your check-in date

#### Total Price Calculation

The app automatically calculates:
- **Number of nights** - Difference between check-out and check-in
- **Total cost** - (Number of nights) × (Price per night)
- Updated in real-time as you change dates

### Uploading Proof of Booking

1. Tap the **image upload area** on the booking page
2. Select an image from your phone gallery:
   - Receipt from payment
   - Confirmation screenshot
   - ID verification

3. The selected image will be displayed in the upload area
4. You can tap to change or remove the image

### Confirming Your Booking

1. Review all booking details carefully:
   - Room name and details
   - Check-in and check-out dates
   - Total price
   - Proof image uploaded

2. Tap **"Confirm Booking"** button
3. A confirmation dialog will appear with:
   - **QR Code** - Unique booking code for check-in
   - **View QR Code** - Button to view the code larger
   - **Continue** - Proceed with the booking

#### QR Code Information

- Your unique QR code is generated upon booking confirmation
- Used for check-in verification at the property
- Can be viewed later in your booking details
- Keep this code safe for your check-in

### Booking Confirmation

After confirmation:
- Your booking is submitted as "Pending"
- You will return to the home page
- An automated confirmation alert will be sent
- Admin will review and update the status within 24 hours

### Viewing Favorites

## Alerts & Notifications

### Accessing Your Alerts

1. Tap the **Alerts icon** in the bottom navigation bar
2. You will see your **Alerts page**
3. A **red badge** on the icon shows your unread alert count

### Types of Alerts

**Automated Alerts:**
- Generated automatically based on booking actions
- Status updates: Approved, Confirmed, Completed, Rejected
- Notifications when admin changes your booking

**Admin Announcements:**
- Messages from property management
- Updates about new rooms or promotions
- Important platform announcements
- Can include images and attachments

### Reading Alerts

Alerts are organized into:
- **Unread Alerts** (at top) - Alerts you haven't viewed
- **Read Alerts** (below) - Previously viewed alerts

#### Alert Information

Each alert shows:
- **Type icon** - Automated or Admin designation
- **Title** - Alert heading
- **Message** - Detailed content
- **Timestamp** - When the alert was sent (e.g., "2 hours ago")
- **Read status** - Blue dot for unread alerts

### Managing Alerts

**Mark as Read:**
1. Tap any unread alert
2. It moves to the "Read Alerts" section
3. The blue indicator dot disappears

**Mark All as Read:**
1. Tap **"Mark all read"** button in the app bar
2. All unread alerts are marked as read
3. Useful for clearing the badge notification

### Understanding Booking Alerts

When your booking status changes:
- You receive an automated alert
- Alert title shows the status update
- Message includes the room name and new status
- Check this section regularly for important updates
